Why do the wave shapes sound different?

Every repeating wave has a fundamental frequency. The higher harmonic peaks occur at whole-number multiples of it: 2f, 3f, 4f, and so on.

Changing the wave shape changes the strengths of those harmonics. The pitch can stay the same even when the sound's character changes.

These plots show ideal mathematical signals, not a recording from your speakers. Actual loudness and high-frequency response depend on your device.
